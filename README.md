## About

I am a Ph.D. candidate at East China Normal University, China. I received the M.Sc. degree in Renewable Energy and Clean Technology from the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at The University of Manchester, U.K., in 2023.

## Research

My work focuses on silicon-photonic transceivers, broadband analog and mixed-signal equalization, and measurement-driven characterization of high-speed electrical and optical links.
